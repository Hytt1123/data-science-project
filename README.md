# Global Energy Transition and Data Compliance Panel Analysis

## 1. Research Problem & Objective
This project examines the multi-dimensional panel relationships among national economic output (GDP per capita), renewable energy adoption, carbon emissions, and environmental data compliance indices across 10 European nations from 2015 to 2023.

## 2. Dataset Source & Licensing
- **Dataset Name**: Global Energy Transition and Data Compliance Panel Dataset (WDI & IRENA derived).
- **Source Framework**: World Bank World Development Indicators (WDI) & International Renewable Energy Agency (IRENA).
- **Licensing**: Open access for academic and research purposes.

## 3. Methods and Tools Used
- **Programming Language**: Python 3.10
- **Libraries**: `pandas`, `matplotlib`, `numpy`
- **Methodology**: Panel data aggregation, multi-dimensional multivariate visualization (bubble charts mapping multiple variables simultaneously and multi-year trend line analysis).

## 4. Key Findings & Limitations
- **Key Finding**: Higher data compliance indices strongly correlate with accelerated renewable energy penetration and structural reduction in carbon emission intensity over the 8-year panel window.
- **Limitation**: Macro-level aggregation may mask sub-national policy disparities and localized energy infrastructure constraints.

## 5. How to Run the Analysis
1. Clone the repository.
2. Install dependencies: `pip install -r requirements.txt`
3. Execute the script: `python analysis.py`
